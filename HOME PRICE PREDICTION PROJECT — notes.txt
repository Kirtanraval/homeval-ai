4. TARGET TRANSFORMATION
   You used:

y = log1p(price)  [mathematically: log(1 + price)]

Why?

House prices vary a lot:
Example:
100k → 2M

Log transformation:

* Reduces extreme values
* Makes model more stable
* Improves accuracy

After prediction:
You converted back using:
expm1()  [mathematically: exp(1 + x) - 1, inverse of log1p]

So final output is real price again.

Note: log1p and expm1 are used because they handle edge cases better than
log and exp functions (e.g., they work with small/zero values).

8. MODEL EVALUATION METRICS

R² Score:
Measures how well model explains price variation.

Range:
0 → bad
1 → perfect

Your score:
~0.86 to 0.88

Meaning:
Model explains ~86–88% of house price patterns.

Very good for a student project.

---

RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error):

This tells:
On average, how much the prediction is off.

Example:
RMSE = 140000
Means:
Prediction error ≈ ±140k dollars

Lower is better.
